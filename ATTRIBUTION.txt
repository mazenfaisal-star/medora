CURRENT MODEL-COVERAGE LIMITATIONS — DO NOT OVERSTATE
- Several brain substructure, heart-part and lung-part views are procedural Three.js educational shapes built from primitives, not anatomical scans or validated individual-part meshes. These are placeholders, not proof of a real organ-specific model.
- The seven brain-region selector entries and the 46 individual-part catalogue entries are not equivalent to 53 verified standalone anatomical models.
- The current 46-part catalogue covers: 11 brain region/structure entries (four lobes, cerebellum, brainstem and five deeper structures), 11 heart structures, 9 respiratory structures and 15 male reproductive structures. Coverage is uneven; the count is a catalogue count, not a model-validation count.
- The male reproductive part set includes some source-backed meshes/viewers, simplified Layered Body Atlas illustrations and at least one disclosed missing model (spermatic cord). It is not complete.
- A model appearing in a viewer is not a clinical validation. Verify anatomical scope, source dataset, sex/age/variant where relevant, mesh labels, licence and specialist review before describing it as anatomically validated.
- Third-party attribution must not be hidden. Medora may use a clean viewer layout, but must preserve creator, dataset and licence information in the project/source credits.

1) Brain Explorer — ScienceQuest:
   https://sciencequest.in/tools/brain-3d-explorer/
   101 selectable brain structures, including frontal/parietal/temporal gyri, hippocampus, amygdala, thalamus, hypothalamus, brainstem structures, corpus callosum and cerebellum. The cortical lobe pages are represented by separately segmented gyri rather than one complete mesh per entire lobe. The cerebellum is one mesh and does not include folia/lobules/deep nuclei.
   Source geometry: BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0; converted and published by ScienceQuest under the same licence. The explorer describes the source as imaging of one adult male body.
   Model page: https://sciencequest.in/tools/brain-3d-explorer/

   Includes separately selectable tricuspid, mitral and pulmonary valves, papillary muscles, coronary arteries and cardiac veins. It explicitly does not include an aortic valve, chamber meshes, or great vessels beyond the coronary ostia. The heart wall is one mesh.
   Source geometry: BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0; converted and published by ScienceQuest under the same licence. One adult male anatomical specimen.
   Model page: https://sciencequest.in/tools/heart-3d-explorer/

   Model page: https://sciencequest.in/tools/great-vessels-3d-explorer/

4) Lungs and Bronchial Tree Explorer — ScienceQuest:

   Source geometry: BodyParts3D 3.0, © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0; simplified, converted and published by ScienceQuest under the same licence. Based on MRI of one adult male volunteer and refined by medical illustrators.
